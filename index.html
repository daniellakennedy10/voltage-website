About the team

Voltage is a student-led FIRST Tech Challenge team based at Flower Mound High School in Flower Mound, Texas. We're a small, hands-on crew that handles our own mechanical design, software, and business operations — no separate build team versus drive team here, everyone touches the robot.

We debuted as team #32655 in our rookie season and picked up hardware right out of the gate. For 2026–27 we're competing as team #27515, a second-year veteran squad with a regional-qualifying season already behind us.

Built to compete. Driven to inspire.
